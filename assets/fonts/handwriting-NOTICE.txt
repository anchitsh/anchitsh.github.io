Name studies use name-only subsets of four EMS single-line typefaces, licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete license is in OFL.txt.

EMS Capitol: derived from Sacramento by Brian J. Bonislawsky, Astigmatic One
Eye Typographic Institute.
EMS Allure: derived from Allura by Rob Leuschke, TypeSETit.
EMS Decorous Script: derived from Petit Formal Script by Impallari Type.
EMS Society: derived from Mrs Saint Delafield by Alejandro Paul, Sudtipos.

EMS single-line adaptations: Sheldon B. Michaels.
SVG font conversion: Windell H. Oskay.

Sources:
https://github.com/golanlevin/p5-single-line-font-resources/tree/main/p5_single_line_svg_fonts/single_line_svg_fonts/EMS
https://github.com/techninja/hersheytextjs/blob/master/svg_fonts/EMSAllure.svg

Only paths for "Anchit Sharma." are included in signature-fonts.js. They are
scaled and gently smoothed for display. Layout and stroke timing are local.
The preview names Capitol, Allure, Decorous, and Society identify these subsets.
No external font request or runtime dependency is required.
