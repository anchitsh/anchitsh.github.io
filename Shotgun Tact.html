What I did

I built Shotgun-Tact during a 48-hour game jam. It is a time-slowing top-down shooter that won first place at WTF x IGDC Jam 2024.

Highlights

  • Implemented the core shooting, time-slowing, and level flow in Unity.
  • Created pixel art and level design under a 48-hour production window.
  • Placed first among more than 340 submissions from a jam with over 1,900 participants.
