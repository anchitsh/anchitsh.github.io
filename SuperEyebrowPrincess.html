Super Eyebrow Princess

Year:
2021
Role:
Programmer/Level Designer
Tech:
Unity, C#
Platforms:
PC
Credits:
Art (Maid), Music (Charlie)

This game was made for a week-long jam in November 2020.

The idea came from flipping a classic trope: the princess saves the knight from the dragon.

We couldn't think of a good name, so we named it after the princess and her angry eyebrows.

We got a lot done in that one week, finishing several polished levels.

There are four levels, plus a final level where you fight the dragon.
