A horror platformer where you're a puppet controlled by a large, mysterious hand.

This game was made in 48 hours for GMTK Game Jam 2020.

Everything that could go wrong during this jam did. We started as a five-person team that met on Discord, but 30 hours in, three members left, leaving just the artist and me.

We didn't give up and finished a barely playable game. It's not much, but I'm proud we finished it!
