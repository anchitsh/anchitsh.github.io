A puzzle platformer about clearing levels by throwing UNO Reverse cards at obstacles to rewind them.

I made this game in under a week for Brackeys Game Jam 2020.2. The theme was "Rewind".

I did all the art and programming myself, so I'm proud of it!

The whole idea came from the UNO Reverse card meme.
